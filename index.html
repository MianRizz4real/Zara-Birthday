Happy Birthday, Zara

Dear Zara — somewhere between the small mornings and the loud, busy days, you became the part I look forward to most. Today isn't really about cake or candles, though we'll have both. It's about pausing, on purpose, to say what I mean to say every day but don't always find the words for.

You have this way of making ordinary moments feel like they matter — a bad day gets softer, a good one gets brighter, just because you're in it. I don't think you know how much steadier I feel simply because you exist in my life.

So here's to another year of your laugh, your ideas, your ridiculous playlists, and the kindness you hand out like it costs nothing. I hope this year gives you everything you're quietly hoping for, and a few things you didn't know to ask for.

Happy birthday, my love. Today is yours — I just get to celebrate it with you.

Forever yours,

— Khuzaima
